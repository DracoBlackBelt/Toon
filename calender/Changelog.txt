1.2.1
- the calendar files are now parsed in a separate thread (WorkerScript), the Toon no longer
  freezes for a few seconds while the calendars are processed. Falls back to processing in the
  interface thread if the worker is not available.
- complete rewrite of the ICS parser and the recurrence handling:
  - all upcoming occurrences of recurring appointments are shown (up to a year ahead, max 224 items)
    instead of only the first one
  - exceptions to a series are handled: deleted occurrences (EXDATE), moved or changed occurrences
    (RECURRENCE-ID) and cancelled occurrences (STATUS:CANCELLED)
  - extra dates in a series (RDATE) are shown
  - monthly/yearly rules on the n-th weekday (e.g. 2nd Tuesday, last Friday), BYMONTHDAY, BYMONTH,
    BYSETPOS and WKST are supported
  - appointments with a DURATION instead of an end time are supported
  - folded (continued) lines and escaped characters (\, \; \n) in titles are handled correctly
  - all-day appointments spanning multiple days show the end date ("t/m dd-mm")
  - reminders (VALARM) are calculated from the actual trigger, also for absolute triggers
  - emoji and other symbols the Toon font cannot show are removed from titles
  - UTF-8 calendars served with a wrong charset (e.g. iso-8859-1) are decoded correctly, no more
    garbled accented letters
- a calendar server that does not answer no longer blocks the other calendars (90 s timeout)
- the list screen refreshes itself when new data arrives and shows a message while loading
- the 'Verversen' button keeps the list screen open
